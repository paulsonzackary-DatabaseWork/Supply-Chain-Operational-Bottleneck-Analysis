#data dictonary

#logistics shipment dataset

| Shipment_ID | Unique identifier for shipments|
| Origin_Warehouse | The warehouse where the product got shipped from |
| Destination | Self explanitory, but it's the destination the product is being sent to |
| Carrier | The company that is responsible for shipping the product |
| Shipment_Date | The date that the product was shipped |
| Delivery_Date | The date that the product arrived |
| Weight_kg | The weight of the shipment in kilograms |
| Cost | The cost of shipment |
| Status | Whether or not the product made it to it's destination or not |
| Distance_miles | the number of miles that it took for the shipment to arrive |
| Transit_days | How many days it took for the product to arrive |
